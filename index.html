01 / INTRO

Hello,
I 'm Lucy Liu.

交互设计师 INTERACTION DESIGNER

关注系统、界面与体验，
以及它们如何共同构成完整的交互。

Interested in systems, interfaces and experiences — and how they work together as a whole.

我习惯从复杂的信息、场景和系统关系出发，通过当代视觉语言与新兴技术， 将它们转化为更直观、清晰且具有吸引力的交互体验。
同时，我也持续探索 交互设计在新兴领域中的可能性，包括 具身智能与 Human–AI 交互、低 空经济及新型服务体验。我希望通过改善用户与产品、服务和系统之间的 互动方式，提升客户与消费者的整体体验。

I approach complex information, contexts, and system relationships through contemporary visual languages and emerging technologies, translating them into intuitive, clear, and engaging experiences.
I also explore how interaction design can shape emerging fields, including embodied intelligence, Human–AI Interaction, low-altitude mobility, and new service experiences. Through better interactions between people, products, services, and systems, I aim to improve both customer and user experiences while creating meaningful value for each project.

02 / HOW I THINK

Good interaction design

starts with understanding the whole system.

好的交互设计，

始于理解整个系统。

WHAT I LOOK AT

Experience is shaped by more than the interface.

我关注什么

体验从来不只由界面本身决定。

01

TECHNOLOGY

Capability · Interaction · Possibility

技术

能力 · 交互 · 可能性

Technology is not only a tool for implementation. It creates new behaviours and possibilities for interaction. I consider which capabilities are meaningful enough to become part of the experience.
技术不仅是实现设计的工具， 它也会创造新的行为方式与交互可能。 我会判断哪些技术能力真正值得 被转化为用户能够理解和使用的体验。
02

USER

Needs · Behaviour · Context

用户

需求 · 行为 · 场景

What are people really trying to achieve? In what context? How do behaviour, emotion and cognition shape the experience?
用户真正想完成什么？ 他们处于什么场景中？ 行为、情绪和认知 会如何影响体验？
CORE
EXPERIENCE
体验
03

SERVICE

Touchpoints · Process · Delivery

服务

触点 · 流程 · 服务实现

The interface is only one part of a service. I look at how digital and physical touchpoints, operational processes and different stages connect into one coherent experience.
用户看到的界面只是服务的一部分。 我会关注线上与线下、不同触点 以及后台流程如何共同构成 完整的体验。
04

SYSTEM

Roles · Information · Flow

系统

角色 · 信息 · 流程

What roles, information, flows and dependencies exist behind the experience? I look at how these parts relate and work together as a system.
一个体验背后有哪些角色、 信息、流程和依赖关系？ 我会理解这些部分之间 如何连接并共同运作。
05

VALUE

User · Client · Real-world Value

价值

用户 · 客户 · 现实价值

Does the design create value for users, clients and the wider system? Is the problem worth solving, and can the solution work in the real world?
设计是否同时为用户、 客户和整个系统创造价值？ 它解决的问题是否值得被解决， 并能够在现实条件下成立？

03 / HOW I DESIGN

From complexity to coherent experiences.

从复杂问题到完整体验。

I move from understanding the problem to structuring the system, crafting interactions and refining the final experience.

从理解问题开始，梳理信息与系统关系， 再将其转化为交互与界面， 并通过验证与迭代形成完整体验。

01

UNDERSTAND

Understand people, contexts and problems.

User Research · Context Research · Personas · User Journey · Pain Points · Competitive Analysis

理解

理解用户、场景与真正需要解决的问题。

用户研究 · 场景研究 · 用户画像 · 用户旅程 · 痛点分析 · 竞品分析
02

STRUCTURE

Turn insights into clear structures, flows and relationships.

Information Architecture · User Flow · Service Flow · Interaction Logic · System Structure · Feature Planning

梳理

将研究洞察转化为清晰的信息结构、 流程与系统关系。

信息架构 · 用户流程 · 服务流程 · 交互逻辑 · 系统结构 · 功能规划
03

CRAFT

Craft interactions into tangible experiences.

Wireframes · Interaction Design · UI Design · Visual System · Motion · Prototypes

设计

线框设计 · 交互设计 · UI 设计 · 视觉系统 · 动效设计 · 交互原型
